This application uses the browser-optimized BodyParts3D 4.0 packaging published by the open-source Human Atlas project: https://github.com/ashemag/human-atlas

Human Atlas adaptations include coordinate/unit conversion for web rendering, browser-oriented geometry simplification, quantized normals, binary chunk packaging, display-system groupings, and colors. Human Atlas reports 2,234 represented source meshes and 3,432 named concepts in the packaged adult male reference.

Human Atlas application code is MIT licensed, Copyright (c) 2026 ashemag. Portions of the 3D rendering architecture in EMT Human Atlas are adapted from that project under the MIT License.

BodyParts3D is a reference anatomy, not a complete representation of every human structure or variation. EMT Human Atlas is an educational simulator and not a diagnostic, surgical, or clinical decision-support tool.
